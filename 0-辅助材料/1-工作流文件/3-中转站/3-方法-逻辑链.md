## 文献1 (PMID: 41240582)

### 化合物ADMET预测（1-生物信息学）
#### Acquisition of atrazine structural information and toxicity prediction
The standardized chemical structure and SMILES notation of Atrazine were retrieved from the PubChem database. Subsequently, the toxicological characteristics of Atrazine were systematically evaluated using the online tools ADMETlab 3.0 and ProTox 3.0, covering multiple toxicological endpoints including carcinogenicity, genotoxicity, neurotoxicity, hepatotoxicity, nephrotoxicity, and environmental ecotoxicity.

### 化合物靶点预测（1-生物信息学）
#### Target prediction of atrazine and collection of CRC-related genes
To identify the potential targets of Atrazine, its SMILES notation was submitted to four publicly available databases. The inclusion thresholds were as follows: STITCH (minimum required interaction score ≥ 0.4), SuperPred (probability ≥ 0.6 and model accuracy ≥ 80 %), SwissTargetPrediction (probability ≥ 0.03), and TargetNet (probability ≥ 0.5). The resulting targets were further processed using the UniProt database to ensure that only Homo sapiens-derived targets were retained.

### 疾病靶点筛选（1-生物信息学）
#### Target prediction of atrazine and collection of CRC-related genes
For CRC-related genes, we systematically retrieved data from multiple resources with defined criteria. In GeneCards, genes with a relevance score ≥ 20 were included. In OMIM, only those recorded as approved symbols were selected. In the Therapeutic Target Database (TTD), the keyword "colorectal cancer" was used, and all identified targets were incorporated without exclusion. All database searches were conducted between January and March 2025.

### 靶点交集筛选 **[待审]**
#### Screening of potential targets and functional enrichment analysis
To investigate the potential molecular mechanisms underlying the relationship between Atrazine and CRC, the ggvenn package in R was employed to identify candidate toxicological targets by extracting the intersection of compound-related targets and disease-associated genes. Based on these intersecting genes, a compound-target-disease ternary network was constructed and visualized using Cytoscape 3.10.2 to elucidate the potential regulatory interactions through which Atrazine may contribute to CRC tumorigenesis and progression.

### GO和KEGG富集分析（1-生物信息学）
#### Screening of potential targets and functional enrichment analysis
Furthermore, GO annotation and KEGG pathway enrichment analyses were conducted using the clusterProfiler package. GO analysis encompassed biological processes, cellular components, and molecular functions. The enrichment results were adjusted using the false discovery rate (FDR), with statistical significance defined as an adjusted P-value < 0.05. Additionally, the Metascape platform was utilized for complementary annotation of the intersecting genes to enhance the comprehensiveness of functional interpretation.

### Bulk转录组数据处理（1-生物信息学）
#### Identification of atrazine-associated core genes in CRC
To identify core genes associated with Atrazine exposure in CRC, transcriptomic datasets of CRC were retrieved from the GEO database based on predefined inclusion criteria. Eligible datasets included tissue-derived samples comprising untreated tumor tissues and corresponding normal tissues, with both experimental and control groups containing no fewer than four samples each. During data preprocessing, the limma package was applied to remove duplicate probes, followed by log2 transformation as appropriate according to the expression distribution characteristics, and normalization was performed using the normalizeBetweenArrays function. For datasets derived from the same microarray platform, batch effect correction was conducted using the ComBat method within the sva package, and correction efficiency was validated via principal component analysis.

### 机器学习建模（1-生物信息学）
#### Identification of atrazine-associated core genes in CRC
Based on the candidate toxicological targets, a total of 112 machine learning models were constructed by combining 12 algorithms, including Lasso, SVM, RF, GBM, and XGBoost. Model performance was systematically evaluated using receiver operating characteristic (ROC) curves, area under the ROC curve (AUC), and confusion matrices in both training and independent validation sets. The model demonstrating optimal predictive performance and stability was selected, and its feature variables were defined as Atrazine-associated core targets in CRC.

### 表达差异Meta分析
#### Expression validation and protein localization analysis of core targets
To validate the differential expression of core targets in CRC, standardized mean difference (SMD) analysis was performed using the meta package in R based on CRC transcriptomic data from the GEO database. Heterogeneity was assessed via the I² statistic and Cochran's Q test. If I² > 50 % or Q test p-value < 0.05, a random-effects model was applied. Otherwise, a fixed-effects model was used when I² ≤ 50 % and p ≥ 0.05. The expression differences of core target genes between tumor and normal tissues were ultimately visualized using forest plots with 95 % confidence intervals (CI).

#### Expression validation and protein localization analysis of core targets
At the protein level, immunohistochemistry (IHC) images of core target proteins in normal colon and CRC tissues were obtained from the Human Protein Atlas (HPA) database. A qualitative comparison was conducted regarding staining intensity, proportion of positive cells, and localization characteristics between normal and CRC tissues.

### 预后列线图构建（1-生物信息学）
#### Construction of a nomogram based on regression model
To evaluate the potential diagnostic value of core target genes in CRC, a logistic regression model was constructed based on the training set expression matrix, and a nomogram was generated using the rms package to quantify the contribution of each core gene to CRC risk prediction. Model performance was calibrated through 1000 bootstrap resampling iterations, and a calibration curve was plotted using the calibrate function to assess the agreement between predicted probabilities and actual incidence rates. Additionally, decision curve analysis was performed using the rmda package to evaluate the net clinical benefit of the model across different risk thresholds, thereby comprehensively assessing its utility and clinical applicability in risk prediction for CRC.

### 分子对接（1-生物信息学）
#### Molecular docking simulation between atrazine and core target proteins
To validate the direct interactions between Atrazine and core target proteins, the standardized chemical structure of Atrazine was obtained from the PubChem database, and three-dimensional structures of the core target proteins were retrieved from the RCSB PDB database. The protein and Atrazine structures were imported into the CB-Dock2 platform for blind docking simulations. The binding affinities between Atrazine and each core target protein were evaluated by calculating vina scores based on the AutoDock Vina algorithm, where lower vina scores indicate more stable interactions and stronger binding affinities. The binding energy matrix was imported into the R environment, and a heatmap was generated using the ggplot2 package to visualize the distribution of binding affinities between Atrazine and different core target proteins.

### GSEA富集分析（1-生物信息学）
#### Gene set enrichment analysis of core target genes
To investigate the functional roles of Atrazine-associated core target genes in CRC and the key signaling pathways they regulate, GSEA was performed based on transcriptomic data from the training set. CRC samples were divided into high-expression and low-expression groups according to the median expression levels of core target genes. Subsequently, log2 fold changes for all genes were calculated and ranked in descending order to construct a ranked gene list. Enrichment analysis was conducted using the clusterProfiler package, with the Hallmark gene sets from the MSigDB database serving as the reference. Pathways with an FDR-adjusted p-value less than 0.05 were considered significantly enriched. Finally, representative pathways closely associated with CRC tumorigenesis and progression were visualized to elucidate the biological processes and signaling networks potentially mediated by the core target genes.

### 免疫浸润分析（1-生物信息学）
#### Immune infiltration analysis and single-cell expression profiling of core target genes
To further elucidate the regulatory roles of Atrazine-associated core target genes within the immune microenvironment of CRC, immune cell infiltration analysis was performed using expression data from the training set. The CIBERSORT algorithm, in conjunction with the LM22 immune cell reference matrix, was applied to quantify the relative abundance of 22 immune cell subtypes in each CRC sample. A permutation number of 1000 was set, and samples with a p-value < 0.05 were retained for subsequent analyses. Spearman correlation analysis was conducted to assess the relationships between core target gene expression levels and the infiltration levels of immune cell subtypes. Based on correlation coefficients and statistical significance, an immune regulatory network was constructed.

### SHAP特征重要性分析（1-生物信息学）
#### Feature importance analysis based on SHAP values
To further evaluate the importance of Atrazine-associated core genes in the CRC prediction model and their contributions to model outputs, this study performed model interpretability analysis using the Shapley Additive exPlanations (SHAP) algorithm. The normalized gene expression matrix and core gene list were imported into the R environment, and sample grouping information was extracted according to the sample naming convention. The dataset was then randomly divided into training and testing sets at a 7:3 ratio, and a five-fold repeated cross-validation strategy was applied to construct the machine learning model. After selecting the algorithm with the highest AUC as the optimal model, the sample grouping variable was binarized, and the model was retrained to obtain stable predictive performance. Subsequently, the kernelshap function was used to calculate the SHAP values of each gene in the model, thereby estimating the relative contribution of each feature to the model output. The resulting SHAP values were visualized using the shapviz package to generate bar plots of feature importance, bee swarm plots, waterfall plots, and individual force plots. Finally, the genes were ranked according to the mean absolute SHAP values, and those with the highest contribution were defined as the most critical core genes mediating Atrazine-induced CRC progression.

### 细胞培养与处理（3-湿实验）

To further evaluate the effects of Atrazine exposure on the biological behavior and key gene expression of CRC cells, in vitro experiments were conducted using human CRC cell lines HCT116 and SW480. Cells were cultured in DMEM medium supplemented with 10 % fetal bovine serum and 1 % penicillin–streptomycin at 37°C in a humidified incubator with 5 % CO₂.

### CCK-8实验（3-湿实验）
#### Cellular function assays and validation of key gene expression
Cell proliferation was assessed using the Cell Counting Kit-8 (CCK-8) assay. When cells reached the logarithmic growth phase, HCT116 and SW480 cells were seeded into 96-well plates at a density of 5 × 10³ cells per well and exposed to DMEM containing different concentrations of Atrazine (0.1 μM and 0.5 μM), with untreated cells serving as the control group. After treatment, 10 μL of CCK-8 reagent was added to each well and incubated for an additional 1 h. The optical density was then measured at 450 nm.

### 细胞划痕实验（3-湿实验）
#### Cellular function assays and validation of key gene expression
Cell migration ability was evaluated by wound healing assay. HCT116 and SW480 cells in the logarithmic growth phase were seeded in 6-well plates. When cell confluence reached 70 %–80 %, scratches were made perpendicularly across the cell monolayer using a 200 μL pipette tip. The wells were rinsed twice with PBS, and cells were then exposed to DMEM medium containing 0.1 μM or 0.5 μM Atrazine for 24 h. Wound closure was observed under a microscope, and the migration rate was quantified using ImageJ software.

### 实时荧光定量PCR（3-湿实验）
#### Cellular function assays and validation of key gene expression
To verify changes in key gene expression after Atrazine exposure, quantitative real-time PCR (qPCR) was performed to detect mRNA levels in different treatment groups. After treatment with varying concentrations and time points of Atrazine, total RNA was extracted using TRIzol reagent and quantified with a NanoDrop 2000 spectrophotometer before being reverse-transcribed into cDNA. PCR amplification was conducted using a SYBR Green system, with β-actin serving as the internal control. The reaction conditions were as follows: pre-denaturation at 95°C for 30 s, followed by 40 cycles of 95°C for 5 s and 60°C for 30 s. Relative gene expression levels were calculated using the 2^−ΔΔCt method. All experiments were performed in triplicate. The primers were synthesized by Nanning Jienisi Biotechnology Co., Ltd., and their sequences are provided in [Supplementary File 1](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325017476?via%3Dihub#sec0160).

### 研究设计（5-通用辅助）
#### Study design
We implemented a multi-stage research framework integrating genetic causality, molecular profiling, cellular mapping, longitudinal population analysis, and mechanistic experimentation to establish CTSH as a putative regulatory and therapeutic node in diabetic retinopathy, with specific genetic prioritization focused on PDR. Discovery analyses were performed using summary-data Mendelian randomization (SMR) integrating eQTLGen and GTEx with PDR GWAS datasets from FinnGen and eight European cohorts. Validation analyses consisted of two-sample MR using independent proteomic datasets (INTERVAL, ARIC, AGES) and Bayesian colocalization to infer shared causal variants. Clinical relevance was assessed using longitudinal proteomic and metabolomic profiling from the UKB. Cellular specificity was evaluated by integrating single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) with cell-type-specific MR (OneK1K, DICE). Functional mechanisms were examined in diabetic mouse models, human ocular tissues, and high-glucose-stimulated monocyte-lineage cells. Finally, structure-based virtual screening and molecular dynamics (MD) simulations were used to evaluate CTSH druggability and identify candidate inhibitors.
#### GWAS datasets
The DGIdb database served as a reference for identifying druggable genes and known drug-gene interactions [42](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B42).

### 两样本孟德尔随机化（1-生物信息学）
#### GWAS datasets
The primary PDR dataset was FinnGen R9 (2,025 cases; 284,826 controls) [40](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B40). Replication was performed using a meta-analysis of eight European cohorts (398 PDR cases; 2,848 controls) [41](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B41). Proteomic quantitative trait locus (pQTL) data were obtained from three independent cohorts: AGES-Reykjavik Study (5,764 older adults, ≥67 years, Iceland) [43](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B43), ARIC Study (15,792 middle-aged adults from four U.S. communities) [44](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B44), and the INTERVAL Study (50,000 UK blood donors, with 3,301 participants profiled for plasma proteomics) [45](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B45).
#### Causal inference and functional annotation approaches
#### Two-Sample Mendelian Randomization
wo-sample MR was conducted using independent SNPs strongly associated with the exposure (p < 5 × 10^-8) as instrumental variables [51](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B51). Variants were clumped for linkage disequilibrium using an r² threshold of < 0.01 within a 10,000-kb window. For each analysis, we recorded the number of retained instruments and calculated instrument strength using the F statistic; instruments with F > 10 were considered sufficiently strong to reduce the likelihood of weak-instrument bias. When only a single valid instrument was available, the Wald ratio was applied; otherwise, the primary method was inverse-variance weighted (IVW), with MR-Egger and weighted median used as sensitivity analyses when instrument number permitted. Horizontal pleiotropy was evaluated using the MR-Egger intercept and MR-PRESSO, and heterogeneity was assessed using Cochran's Q test [52](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B52). Analyses were implemented in R with the TwoSampleMR package. Detailed instrument numbers and corresponding statistics for each analysis are provided in the Supplementary Tables. Significance was defined as p < 0.05.

Colocalization analysis was performed using the coloc package to estimate posterior probabilities for five hypotheses (PP0-PP4), with PP4 representing the probability that the exposure and outcome share a common causal variant. Regional colocalization was interpreted jointly with MR effect estimates and instrument characteristics to reduce overinterpretation of isolated associations.

### 单细胞RNA测序数据处理（1-生物信息学）
#### ScRNA-seq data processing and Sc-MR analyses
ScRNA-seq datasets derived from fibrovascular membrane (FM) and blood samples from DR patients were retrieved from the Gene Expression Omnibus under accession numbers [GSE165784](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE165784), [GSE248284](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE248284), and [GSE245561](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE245561) [57](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B57)-[59](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B59). Cells with fewer than 200 or more than 7,500 detected genes, or with > 20% mitochondrial gene expression, were excluded. The filtered count matrices were normalized using the SCTransform algorithm in Seurat (version 5.0.3) [60](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B60). Highly variable genes (n = 2,000) were selected for dimensionality reduction using principal component analysis (PCA), and the number of principal components used for downstream analyses (n = 15) was determined based on ElbowPlot inspection. To account for batch effects across datasets and donors, data integration was performed using the Harmony algorithm (v1.2.0). Integration quality was evaluated using multiple complementary metrics. Batch mixing and integration performance were assessed using the k-nearest neighbor batch effect test (kBET) and the Local Inverse Simpson's Index (LISI). kBET rejection rates were calculated to quantify batch mixing across integrated datasets, while integration LISI (iLISI) scores were used to evaluate donor mixing across cell populations. Donor contribution to each cell cluster was additionally examined to ensure that no individual donor dominated cluster composition after integration.

Unsupervised clustering was carried out by constructing a Shared Nearest Neighbor (SNN) graph using Seurat's FindNeighbors function, followed by clustering with the FindClusters function at a resolution of 0.6. Non-linear dimensionality reduction was conducted using Uniform Manifold Approximation and Projection (UMAP) or t-distributed Stochastic Neighbor Embedding (t-SNE), via Seurat's RunUMAP and RunTSNE functions, respectively. Cell types were annotated based on differentially expressed genes (DEGs) identified using the FindAllMarkers function. Marker genes and DEGs were used to define canonical immune subsets and to explore transcriptional differences.

### 生存分析（5-通用辅助）
#### Proteomic and metabolomic analyses in the UKB cohort
#### Cohort and data curation
This analysis utilized the prospective UKB cohort [63](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B63). DR diagnoses were derived from linked hospital records, screening data, and ophthalmic procedure codes. Data curation included removing consent withdrawals, excluding samples with missing key covariates, filtering technical outliers (> 5 SD), and imputing values below the limit of detection. Continuous variables were log- or z-transformed. Time-to-event data were generated with censoring at death, loss to follow-up, or administrative end. For proteomic analyses, the platform-normalized protein abundance values provided by UK Biobank were used as the starting input. CTSH levels were standardized to z-scores for regression and survival analyses to facilitate effect-size comparability across models. Continuous covariates with skewed distributions were log-transformed where appropriate before standardization. Values below the limit of detection were imputed according to the assay-specific preprocessing pipeline, and samples with extreme technical values (> 5 SD from the mean) were excluded as outliers.
#### Longitudinal risk modeling
CTSH (modeled both continuously and by quantiles) was entered as the exposure in Cox proportional hazards models for time to incident DR. Sequential models were specified to improve transparency. The core model adjusted for age, sex, ethnicity, socioeconomic deprivation, smoking status, alcohol intake, body mass index, systolic blood pressure, antihypertensive medication use, lipid-lowering medication use, diabetes status, and relevant technical covariates related to proteomic measurement. The extended model additionally incorporated available metabolic traits, including HbA1c, where available in the corresponding analytic subset. In diabetes-restricted analyses, we further adjusted for baseline glycemic status and major cardiometabolic covariates, but diabetes duration and insulin therapy were not consistently available for all included participants and therefore were not uniformly incorporated into the primary models; this was considered in the interpretation of results. Proportional hazards were checked using Schoenfeld residuals. Kaplan-Meier curves with log-rank tests were generated, and time-dependent ROC-AUCs at 3, 5, and 10 years together with Harrell's C-index were used to quantify model discrimination. Missing covariates were handled using multiple imputation by chained equations (MICE). Twenty imputed datasets were generated, and model estimates were combined using Rubin's rules. The imputation model included the exposure, outcome indicator, follow-up time, and all covariates used in the fully adjusted analyses.
