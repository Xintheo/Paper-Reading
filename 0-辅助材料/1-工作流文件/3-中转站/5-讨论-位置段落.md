| 1 | Acyl-CoA thioesterase 8 (ACOT8) is an enzyme that catalyzes the hydrolysis of fatty acyl-CoA esters into free fatty acids and coenzyme A [15,16](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41905213/). | 2 | Notably, ACOT8 expression is responsive to lipid load. In experimental models of fatty acid overload, cells upregulate ACOT8 [19](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41905213/).
