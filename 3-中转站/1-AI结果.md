### 第1段 逻辑链

候选物质论证

### 第1段 句子标注

1. [B1] [Aspartame](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/pharmacology-toxicology-and-pharmaceutical-science/aspartame) is a synthetic [dipeptide](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/pharmacology-toxicology-and-pharmaceutical-science/dipeptide) that results from the reaction of l-aspartic acid with l-phenylalanine methyl ester, and it has become one of the most widely used [artificial sweeteners](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/pharmacology-toxicology-and-pharmaceutical-science/sweetening-agent) around the world [Choudhary and Pretorius, 2017](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325004257?via%3Dihub#bib8).
2. [B2] Due to its sweetness being approximately 200 times that of [sucrose](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/biochemistry-genetics-and-molecular-biology/sucrose) and its extremely low-calorie content, aspartame is widely used in sugar-free or low-sugar foods and beverages.
3. [C4] Although aspartame has gained widespread use in the market, its safety has been a subject of controversy for many years [Czarnecka et al., 2021](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325004257?via%3Dihub#bib9).
4. [B5] Studies have shown that aspartame and its metabolites can affect cognitive functions in [animal models](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/biochemistry-genetics-and-molecular-biology/animal-model) and humans, including issues with learning, [headaches](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/pharmacology-toxicology-and-pharmaceutical-science/headache), depression, and anxiety.
5. [A10] This may be closely related to the imbalance of excitatory and inhibitory [neurotransmitters](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/pharmacology-toxicology-and-pharmaceutical-science/neurotransmitter) caused by the [aspartic acid](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/biochemistry-genetics-and-molecular-biology/aspartic-acid) released from aspartame [Dar, 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325004257?via%3Dihub#bib11).
6. [A10] Secondly, aspartame can also activate [glutamate receptors](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/pharmacology-toxicology-and-pharmaceutical-science/glutamate-receptor), thereby inducing [neurotoxicity](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/pharmacology-toxicology-and-pharmaceutical-science/neurotoxicity) and neurostimulation[Shaher et al., 2023](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325004257?via%3Dihub#bib37).
7. [C2] Soheila Kashanian [Kashanian et al., 2013](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325004257?via%3Dihub#bib19) conducted in vitro experimental studies and found that aspartame interacts with native calf thymus DNA (CT-DNA), thereby affecting DNA’s regulatory role in cellular structure and function.
8. [C2] Additionally, consuming aspartame during pregnancy can impair [placental growth](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/biochemistry-genetics-and-molecular-biology/placenta-development) in pregnant mice [Huang et al., 2023](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325004257?via%3Dihub#bib17).
9. [C2] Hojat has found that long-term consumption of aspartame leads to a decrease in [serum concentrations](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/biochemistry-genetics-and-molecular-biology/blood-level) of pituitary-testicular axis hormones and induces testicular [oxidative stress](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/biochemistry-genetics-and-molecular-biology/oxidative-stress) and cell apoptosis, thereby causing [male reproductive toxicity](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/biochemistry-genetics-and-molecular-biology/male-reproductive-toxicity) [Anbara et al., 2021](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325004257?via%3Dihub#bib2).

### 第2段 逻辑链

影响因素深入

### 第2段 句子标注

1. [A10] However, by increasing the production of [reactive oxygen species](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/pharmacology-toxicology-and-pharmaceutical-science/reactive-oxygen-metabolite) (ROS), aspartame may further contribute to carcinogenic risks [Shaher et al., 2023](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325004257?via%3Dihub#bib37), including a significant impact on the liver. [Aspartame](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/biochemistry-genetics-and-molecular-biology/aspartame) could activate TP53, leading to impaired [gluconeogenesis](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/biochemistry-genetics-and-molecular-biology/gluconeogenesis) in the liver, and may potentially induce [hepatocellular carcinoma](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/pharmacology-toxicology-and-pharmaceutical-science/liver-cell-carcinoma) independently of its association with non-alcoholic fatty liver disease [Sergi, 2023](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325004257?via%3Dihub#bib36).
2. [C2] Besides, aspartame acts as a carcinogen in experimental animals, significantly increasing the likelihood of liver cancer [Soffritti, 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325004257?via%3Dihub#bib39).
3. [C2] Through an epidemiological cohort study, Charlotte Debras identified aspartame as a risk factor for obesity-related cancers, among which liver cancer is included [Debras et al., 2022](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325004257?via%3Dihub#bib12).
4. [C13] In summary, the aforementioned studies reveal that aspartame exhibits a notably significant carcinogenic potential for the liver.
5. [E2] This study integrates new technologies such as network toxicology and [Mendelian randomization](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/biochemistry-genetics-and-molecular-biology/mendelian-randomization) to construct an innovative research methodology aimed at more systematically evaluating the toxicity and potential mechanisms of artificial sweeteners.
6. [D6] This multidisciplinary research strategy not only provides a scientific interpretation of the public health safety of artificial sweeteners but also opens new technical pathways for the toxicological research of aspartame.
7. [D3] With the increasing use of artificial sweeteners by the population, the awareness of the safety and reliability of aspartame and other artificial sweeteners must be improved.

### 第3段 句子标注

[C9] ProTox 3.0 is a web server for the prediction of toxicity of chemicals, which uses molecular similarity and machine learning models to predict 61 toxicity endpoints, such as [acute toxicity](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/pharmacology-toxicology-and-pharmaceutical-science/acute-toxicity), [organ toxicity](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/pharmacology-toxicology-and-pharmaceutical-science/organ-toxicity), and [adverse reaction](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/pharmacology-toxicology-and-pharmaceutical-science/adverse-event) pathways [Banerjee et al., 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325004257?via%3Dihub#bib4).
2. [C9] As an integrated online platform for accurate and comprehensive predictions of [ADMET](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/pharmacology-toxicology-and-pharmaceutical-science/absorption-distribution-metabolism-excretion-toxicity) properties, ADMETlab 2.0 can predict the [pharmacokinetic](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/biochemistry-genetics-and-molecular-biology/pharmacokinetics) and toxicity characteristics of compounds [Xiong et al., 2021](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325004257?via%3Dihub#bib47).
3. [E6] Consequently, we used the two prediction platforms with different algorithms to predict the toxicity and absorption of aspartame in the human body, providing a more comprehensive understanding of its impact on human health.
4. [C9] Network toxicology, by integrating fields such as systems biology, bioinformatics, and [computational toxicology](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/pharmacology-toxicology-and-pharmaceutical-science/computational-toxicology), conducts a comprehensive evaluation of the impact of chemical substances on biological safety [Liu et al., 2016](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325004257?via%3Dihub#bib24).
5. [E6] Thus, we introduced network toxicology to identify the primary pathways and targets through which aspartame exerts its carcinogenic effects.

### 第4段 句子标注

[E6] MR, a method using [genetic variants](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/biochemistry-genetics-and-molecular-biology/genetic-divergence) as instrumental variables to investigate the effect of exposure on outcomes [Birney, 2022](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325004257?via%3Dihub#bib5).

[E6] The genetic variants used in MR are randomly assigned and determined at conception [You et al., 2021](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325004257?via%3Dihub#bib48), making the results of MR analysis highly robust to confounding factors and reverse causality, which are common in traditional observational studies [Gao et al., 2023](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325004257?via%3Dihub#bib16).

5. [E5] MD simulations are employed to evaluate the ligand-receptor complex, assessing the binding stability and adaptability of the active compounds to the therapeutic targets [Liu et al., 2023](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325004257?via%3Dihub#bib25).

### 第1段 句子标注

1. [A1] Colorectal cancer (CRC) is the third most commonly diagnosed malignancy and the second leading cause of cancer-related death worldwide, posing a significant public health threat to human health [Bray et al., 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325017476?via%3Dihub#bib12).
2. [A6] According to GLOBOCAN 2022 data, there were over 1.92 million new CRC cases and more than 900,000 deaths annually [Bray et al., 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325017476?via%3Dihub#bib12).
3. [A7] The incidence of CRC continues to rise year by year, with a noticeable trend toward younger onset [Akimoto et al., 2021](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325017476?via%3Dihub#bib4).
4. [A12] Despite advancements in early screening and comprehensive treatment strategies, most CRC patients are diagnosed at an advanced stage, resulting in unsatisfactory overall survival rates [Carbone et al., 17 2025](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325017476?via%3Dihub#bib15).
5. [A8] The tumorigenesis and progression of CRC involve complex interactions among multiple factors, including genetic mutations, epigenetic modifications, chronic inflammation, gut microbiota dysbiosis, unhealthy lifestyles, and environmental exposures ([Jin et al., Jun 2025](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325017476?via%3Dihub#bib27), [Murphy et al., Oct 2019](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325017476?via%3Dihub#bib42), [Raskov et al., Jan 2020](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325017476?via%3Dihub#bib46)).
6. [C10] Among these, increasing attention has been paid to environmental factors, particularly the role of exogenous chemical pollutants.
7. [C2] Numerous studies have demonstrated that environmental pollutants can disrupt intestinal microenvironment homeostasis and promote carcinogenesis by inducing oxidative stress, DNA damage, inflammatory responses, endocrine disruption, and immune evasion [Rio et al., 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325017476?via%3Dihub#bib49).
8. [D3] Therefore, elucidating the mechanisms by which environmental toxins contribute to CRC pathogenesis is crucial for advancing etiological understanding and developing effective prevention strategies for CRC.

### 第2段 逻辑链

影响因素深入

### 第2段 句子标注

1. [B1] Atrazine is one of the most widely used triazine herbicides globally, and has been extensively applied in agriculture and horticulture since the 1950s.
2. [B1] It is now recognized as a typical persistent organic pollutant due to its widespread presence in soil, water, and agricultural products [Deng et al., 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325017476?via%3Dihub#bib20).
3. [B2] Atrazine exhibits high environmental stability and bioaccumulation potential, facilitating its entry into the human body via drinking water, the food chain, and inhalation pathways [Zhao et al., 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325017476?via%3Dihub#bib65).
4. [B5] Existing studies have confirmed that Atrazine is a classic endocrine-disrupting chemical, which disrupts the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal axis, impairing sex hormone synthesis and metabolism, consequently inducing reproductive toxicity and developmental disorders ([Guimarães-Ervilha et al., Mar 2025](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325017476?via%3Dihub#bib22), [Wirbisky and Freeman, Dec 2015](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325017476?via%3Dihub#bib61)).
5. [B5] Additionally, Atrazine has been found to exhibit hepatotoxicity, immunotoxicity, and neurotoxicity, with growing concerns regarding its carcinogenicity ([Liu et al., Jun 2021](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325017476?via%3Dihub#bib38), [Liu et al., Jan 2017](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325017476?via%3Dihub#bib37), [Stradtman and Freeman, Aug 31 2021](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325017476?via%3Dihub#bib55)).
6. [C2] Epidemiological investigations have shown that long-term exposure to Atrazine significantly increases the risk of malignancies, particularly hormone-dependent tumors such as breast cancer, prostate cancer, and epithelial ovarian cancer ([Chen et al., Apr 2021](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325017476?via%3Dihub#bib16), [Hu et al., May 2016](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325017476?via%3Dihub#bib26), [Remigio et al., Feb 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325017476?via%3Dihub#bib47), [Wang et al., Mar 15 2023](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325017476?via%3Dihub#bib56)).
7. [D1] Despite growing concerns, studies exploring the association between Atrazine and gastrointestinal malignancies remain limited and preliminary, lacking systematic investigations into the underlying molecular mechanisms.
8. [D3] In light of Atrazine’s widespread distribution and its potential carcinogenic properties, there is a critical need to leverage advanced bioinformatics and systems toxicology methodologies to elucidate the precise molecular pathways and key targets through which Atrazine contributes to the initiation and progression of CRC.

### 第3段 逻辑链

方法与目的

### 第3段 句子标注

1. [D5] This study systematically illuminated the potential molecular mechanisms by which Atrazine contributes to CRC tumorigenesis and progression through an integrated network toxicology approach, incorporating target prediction, functional enrichment analysis, transcriptomic data mining, machine learning-based core gene identification, molecular docking simulations, and immune microenvironment characterization.
2. [E5] Initially, potential targets of Atrazine were predicted through multiple databases and intersected with CRC-related genes to identify candidate toxicological targets.

5. [E5] Molecular docking techniques were applied to validate the binding affinity between Atrazine and core target proteins.
6. [E5] Furthermore, gene set enrichment analysis (GSEA), CIBERSORT-based immune infiltration analysis, and single-cell transcriptomic profiling were utilized to comprehensively explore the functional roles and immune regulatory effects of core target genes.
7. [D5] Collectively, this study aimed to construct a multi-pathway regulatory network of "compound-target-disease" mediated by Atrazine, elucidate its multi-target synergistic carcinogenic mechanisms, and provide a theoretical foundation and potential intervention targets for advancing early warning and precision prevention of CRC.

### 引言类型

### 第1段 逻辑链

疾病问题引出

### 第1段 句子标注

1. [A1] Diabetic retinopathy (DR) is a leading cause of irreversible vision loss in working-age adults worldwide and reflects chronic microvascular injury induced by prolonged hyperglycemia [1,2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B1).
2. [A3] Proliferative diabetic retinopathy (PDR), the most vision-threatening stage of DR, is characterized by pathological neovascularization and fibrovascular membrane formation at the vitreoretinal interface [3,4](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B3).
3. [B4] Advances in metabolic control, panretinal photocoagulation, and intravitreal anti-VEGF therapy have transformed the management of DR [5](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B5)-[7](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B7).
4. [D1] Many patients exhibit incomplete or transient responses, relapse rapidly, or remain refractory to VEGF blockade.
5. [D3] These limitations underscore an urgent need to identify upstream molecular regulators that integrate inflammatory, metabolic, and hypoxic signaling beyond VEGF alone [8](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B8).

### 第2段 逻辑链

机制铺陈

### 第2段 句子标注

1. [A10b] The pathogenesis of PDR results from the convergence of metabolic dysregulation, innate immune activation, and hypoxia-driven angiogenesis within the diabetic retina [9](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B9)-[12](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B12).
2. [A10] Chronic hyperglycemia perturbs mitochondrial function and redox homeostasis, generating reactive oxygen species and promoting capillary nonperfusion [13](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B13).
3. [A10b] Local ischemia stabilizes hypoxia-inducible transcription factors (HIFs) and triggers pro-angiogenic programs that promote endothelial proliferation, extracellular matrix remodeling, and formation of fragile neovessels [14](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B14)-[17](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B17).
4. [A10b] In parallel, monocytes, macrophages, microglia, and dendritic cells become activated and infiltrate the retina, releasing IL-1β, IL-6, TNF-α, chemokines, and proteases that amplify vascular leakage and endothelial dysfunction [18](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B18)-[23](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B23).
5. [A10] Metabolic rewiring, including a glycolytic shift, lipid remodeling, and accumulation of hypoxia-linked metabolites, further reinforces inflammatory activation and HIF-dependent angiogenesis [24](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B24)-[29](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B29).
6. [C13] Collectively, these interconnected pathways create a self-propagating cycle in which metabolic stress, inflammation, and angiogenesis perpetuate retinal injury [30,31](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B30).

### 第3段 逻辑链

候选物质论证

### 第3段 句子标注

1. [D1] Recent genetic studies have begun to uncover risk loci for DR, but many associated variants reside in noncoding regions or map to genes of uncertain retinal relevance [32,33](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B32).
2. [C9] Integrative multi-omics approaches, including expression, protein, and metabolite quantitative trait loci (QTLs) and Mendelian randomization (MR), now enable the identification of molecular drivers with putative causal roles [34,35](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B34).
3. [C10] Within this framework, lysosomal proteases have emerged as potential regulators linking immunity, metabolism, and extracellular remodeling [36](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B36).
4. [B1] Cathepsin H (CTSH), a lysosomal aminopeptidase with immunometabolic and proteolytic functions, is particularly compelling [37](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B37).
5. [B2] CTSH participates in antigen processing, extracellular matrix degradation, cytokine regulation, and inflammatory activation [38](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B38), and recent evidence implicates cathepsin family members, including CTSH, in diabetic inflammation, immune dysregulation, and angiogenesis [39](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13182238/#B39).
6. [D4] Given these features, CTSH represents a plausible upstream node that may integrate metabolic stress, innate immune activation, and HIF/VEGF-driven angiogenesis in PDR.
7. [D1] However, its causal relevance, cellular specificity, and mechanistic contributions to retinal pathology remain unknown.

### 第4段 逻辑链

研究方案陈述

### 第4段 句子标注

1. [D5] In this study, we applied a multi-stage, multi-omics strategy to identify and validate causal regulators of advanced DR, with genetic discovery focused on PDR.
2. [E3] We further leveraged UK Biobank (UKB) proteomics and metabolomics, multiple scRNA-seq datasets, diabetic mouse models, human ocular tissues, and _in vitro_ perturbation assays to elucidate the molecular, cellular, and functional roles of CTSH across DR-related inflammatory, hypoxic, and angiogenic processes.
3. [E3] We further leveraged UKB proteomics and metabolomics, multiple scRNA-seq datasets, diabetic mouse models, human ocular tissues and _in vitro_ perturbation assays to elucidate the molecular, cellular and functional roles of CTSH in PDR pathogenesis.
4. [E5] Finally, structure-based virtual screening and molecular modeling were used to assess CTSH druggability and nominate Eriocitrin as a lead CTSH-targeting candidate with predicted binding to CTSH, which we evaluated _in vivo_ by intravitreal administration in db/db mice benchmarked against anti-VEGF therapy using longitudinal ERG/OCTA/OCT endpoints and terminal retinal molecular profiling.
5. [H1b] Together, these findings position CTSH as a mechanistic biomarker and a promising therapeutic target that bridges metabolic, inflammatory and angiogenic pathways in PDR.
