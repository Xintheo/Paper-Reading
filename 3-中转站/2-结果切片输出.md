## 文献1 (PMID: 41905213)

### 3.1. Mendelian randomization prioritizes causal proteins for acute kidney injury

#### 切片
(两样本孟德尔随机化)[目的][森林图]: To investigate the causal effects of circulating plasma proteins on AKI, we conducted a pQTL-based two-sample Mendelian randomization analysis.
(两样本孟德尔随机化)[举数][森林图]: This approach identified 456 genes whose encoded proteins showed significant putative causal relationships with AKI.
(两样本孟德尔随机化)[举数][森林图]: Among these, 390 genes were categorized as protective factors, while 66 were classified as risk factors (Table S1).
(两样本孟德尔随机化)[比较][森林图]: Specifically, MR estimates suggested that genetically predicted higher levels of proteins such as AIFM1, APOA4, ARID1 A, ATAD2, FOLR1, GRIA4, HEPHL1, HMGB1, HMGB3, HS3ST3B1, IFNA2, IL16, MDM4, NCR3, PSMB5, SPAG11B, TRAPPC3, UBE2G2, UBE2M, USP25, ZAP70, and ZHX3 acted as protective factors (OR < 1).
(两样本孟德尔随机化)[比较][森林图]: In contrast, proteins including HERC5, LCT, and WFIKKN2 were identified as risk factors (OR > 1) ([Fig. 1](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1567576926004030?via%3Dihub#f0005)A-B).
(两样本孟德尔随机化)[验证][森林图]: The estimated effects of the instrumental variables on the outcome further supported these findings ([Fig. 1](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1567576926004030?via%3Dihub#f0005)C).
(两样本孟德尔随机化)[验证][散点图]: Additionally, randomness testing confirmed that the MR analysis adhered to the principle of random assortment under Mendel's second law ([Fig. 1](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1567576926004030?via%3Dihub#f0005)D).
(两样本孟德尔随机化)[操作][森林图]: To validate the robustness of our MR results, we performed sensitivity analyses, including assessments of heterogeneity, horizontal pleiotropy and leave-one-out validation ([Fig. 1](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1567576926004030?via%3Dihub#f0005)E, Table S2).
(GO和KEGG富集分析)[目的][柱状图]: To elucidate the biological functions and signaling pathways associated with these candidate genes, we performed functional enrichment analysis using the Metascape platform.
(GO和KEGG富集分析)[归纳][柱状图]: The results revealed significant enrichment in key processes and pathways such as cytokine-cytokine receptor interaction, proteasome-mediated degradation, adaptive immune system regulation, cell activation, and inflammatory response ([Fig. 1](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1567576926004030?via%3Dihub#f0005)F-H).
(GO和KEGG富集分析)[归纳][柱状图]: These findings suggest that the identified candidate genes may play pivotal roles in the pathogenesis of AKI, potentially through modulating immune and inflammatory pathways.

### 3.2. Identification of candidate genes in IRI through integrated multi-dataset analysis and machine learning

#### 切片
(Bulk转录组数据处理)[目的][散点图]: Building on the MR findings, to identify candidate genes implicated in renal IRI, we integrated and analyzed multiple transcriptomic datasets from the GEO database, including GSE98622, GSE151260, GSE165876, GSE192883, and GSE278358.
(Bulk转录组数据处理)[操作][散点图]: After merging the datasets and correcting batch effects, we obtained a combined cohort comprising kidney tissues from 21 Sham controls and 51 IRI samples ([Fig. 2](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1567576926004030?via%3Dihub#f0010)A).
(差异表达分析)[举数][基因热图]: Comparative analysis revealed 5604 DEGs, of which 3879 were upregulated and 1725 were downregulated in IRI compared to Sham controls (Table S3).
(差异表达分析)[操作][基因热图]: A heatmap displaying the top-ranked DEGs is shown in [Fig. 2](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1567576926004030?via%3Dihub#f0010)B.
(差异表达分析)[目的][韦恩图]: To prioritize genes with concurrent evidence from both transcriptomic changes and genetic causality, we intersected the DEGs with the putative causal genes identified from the MR analysis.
(差异表达分析)[举数][韦恩图]: This integration yielded 46 overlapping genes: 13 that were upregulated in IRI and had an MR odds ratio (OR) > 1, and 33 that were downregulated with an MR OR < 1 ([Fig. 2](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1567576926004030?via%3Dihub#f0010)C, Table S4).
(机器学习建模)[操作][LASSO交叉验证曲线]: We then applied two independent machine learning algorithms to refine the candidate gene list.
(机器学习建模)[举数][LASSO交叉验证曲线]: First, LASSO regression analysis on the 46 overlapping genes identified an optimal subset of 10 feature genes ([Fig. 2](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1567576926004030?via%3Dihub#f0010)D).
(机器学习建模)[举数][特征选择图]: Concurrently, the SVM-RFE (Support Vector Machine-Recursive Feature Elimination) algorithm was applied to the same candidate set, selecting 6 feature genes ([Fig. 2](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1567576926004030?via%3Dihub#f0010)E).
(机器学习建模)[操作][韦恩图]: By intersecting the results from these two methods, we identified four consensus candidate genes: AIFM1, ACOT8, GRB7, and RAB17 ([Fig. 2](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1567576926004030?via%3Dihub#f0010)F).
(机器学习建模)[收束][ROC曲线]: The diagnostic efficacy of these four candidate genes was evaluated using ROC analysis, which confirmed their strong discriminatory power in distinguishing IRI samples from Sham controls ([Fig. 2](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1567576926004030?via%3Dihub#f0010)G).

### 3.4. scRNA-seq analysis: cell annotation and functional enrichment

#### 切片
(单细胞RNA测序数据处理)[目的][降维散点图]: To further elucidate the cellular mechanisms underlying renal ischemia-reperfusion injury, we performed scRNA-seq on mouse kidney tissues at post-operative day 1 (POD1).
(单细胞RNA测序数据处理)[操作][降维散点图]: After quality control and doublet removal, unsupervised clustering revealed 30 transcriptionally distinct cell populations, visualized using UMAP ([Fig. 4](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1567576926004030?via%3Dihub#f0020)A-B).
(单细胞RNA测序数据处理)[操作][降维散点图]: These clusters were annotated into 13 major canonical cell types based on established marker genes: proximal tubule cells (PT), thick ascending limb cells (TAL), thin limb cells (TL), distal convoluted tubule cells (DCT), collecting duct principal cells (CD-PC), collecting duct intercalated cells (CD-IC), endothelial cells (EC), smooth muscle cells (SMC), macrophages, neutrophils, T cells, B cells, and podocytes ([Fig. 4](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1567576926004030?via%3Dihub#f0020)C).
(单细胞RNA测序数据处理)[验证][点图]: A dot plot illustrated the specific expression patterns of key marker genes across all annotated cell types, confirming the identity of each population ([Fig. 4](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1567576926004030?via%3Dihub#f0020)D).
(单细胞RNA测序数据处理)[比较][堆叠柱状图]: Comparative analysis of cellular composition between Sham and IRI groups revealed significant alterations in the proportions of major cell types ([Fig. 4](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1567576926004030?via%3Dihub#f0020)E).
(单细胞RNA测序数据处理)[操作][降维基因分布点图]: Focusing on proximal tubule cells (PTCs), which are highly susceptible to ischemic injury, we performed differential expression analysis between IRI and Sham conditions ([Fig. 4](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1567576926004030?via%3Dihub#f0020)F-G).
(GO和KEGG富集分析)[目的][富集条形图]: To interpret the functional implications of these transcriptional changes, we conducted KEGG and GO enrichment analyses on the DEGs.
(GO和KEGG富集分析)[归纳][富集条形图]: KEGG pathway analysis indicated that upregulated genes in PTCs were significantly enriched in processes such as Salmonella infection, protein processing in the endoplasmic reticulum, chronic myeloid leukemia, endocytosis, and the MAPK signaling pathway.
(GO和KEGG富集分析)[归纳][富集条形图]: In contrast, downregulated genes were prominently enriched in metabolic pathways, including oxidative phosphorylation, carbon metabolism, chemical carcinogenesis - reactive oxygen species, non-alcoholic fatty liver disease and diabetic cardiomyopathy ([Fig. 4](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1567576926004030?via%3Dihub#f0020)H, Table S5).
(GO和KEGG富集分析)[验证][富集条形图]: GO biological process analysis further supported these findings.
(GO和KEGG富集分析)[归纳][富集条形图]: Upregulated genes were primarily involved in ribonucleoprotein complex biogenesis and RNA splicing-related processes.
(GO和KEGG富集分析)[归纳][富集条形图]: Conversely, downregulated genes showed strong enrichment in fundamental mitochondrial and bioenergetic functions, such as the small molecule catabolic process, cellular respiration, energy derivation by oxidation of organic compounds, aerobic respiration and organic acid catabolic process ([Fig. 4](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1567576926004030?via%3Dihub#f0020)I, Table S6).
(GO和KEGG富集分析)[收束][富集条形图]: Collectively, these results demonstrate that IRI induces a transcriptional shift in PTCs, activating stress-response pathways while suppressing mitochondrial oxidative metabolism, suggesting a state of metabolic reprogramming that may contribute to tubular injury.

(免疫组化)[归纳][组织染色图]: Its overexpression coincides with renal lipid accumulation, suggesting a potential role in driving lipotoxicity.

(酶联免疫吸附测定)[比较][柱状图]: Again, supplementation with exogenous PA reversed the cytokine changes induced by both ACOT8 knockdown and overexpression ([Fig. 7](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1567576926004030?via%3Dihub#f0035)J-L).
(酶联免疫吸附测定)[收束][柱状图]: These results demonstrate that ACOT8 regulates PA levels in tubular epithelial cells following injury.
(酶联免疫吸附测定)[归纳][柱状图]: This modulation of the local lipid milieu influences macrophage polarization, promoting a pro-inflammatory M1 phenotype through a PA-dependent mechanism.

### 3.9. ACOT8-mediated palmitate induces mtDNA release and activates cGAS-STING to promote M1 polarization

#### 切片
(线粒体功能检测)[目的][流式散点图]: Given that cGAS is a cytosolic DNA sensor, we hypothesized that PA derived from ACOT8-overexpressing tubular cells induces mitochondrial damage in macrophages, leading to mtDNA leakage and consequent STING activation.
(线粒体功能检测)[操作][流式散点图]: We first assessed mitochondrial membrane potential using JC-1 staining.
(线粒体功能检测)[比较][流式散点图]: Flow cytometry revealed that both direct PA treatment and CM from H/R-treated HK-2 cells transfected with control siRNA significantly disrupted membrane potential.
(线粒体功能检测)[比较][柱状图]: Notably, CM from si-ACOT8 HK-2 cells partially restored the membrane potential compared to the si-NC group ([Fig. 9](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1567576926004030?via%3Dihub#f0045)A-B).
(线粒体功能检测)[比较][柱状图]: Consistently, ATP production assays demonstrated that PA treatment and si-NC CM markedly reduced intracellular ATP levels, whereas this reduction was significantly ameliorated in macrophages cultured with si-ACOT8 CM ([Fig. 9](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1567576926004030?via%3Dihub#f0045)C).
(胞质线粒体DNA检测)[目的][柱状图]: We next examined whether this mitochondrial dysfunction led to mtDNA release into the cytosol.
