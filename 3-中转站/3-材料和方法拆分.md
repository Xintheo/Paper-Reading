## 细胞培养与处理-CCK-8实验-细胞划痕实验-实时荧光定量PCR

- PMID: 41240582

### 细胞培养与处理

To further evaluate the effects of Atrazine exposure on the biological behavior and key gene expression of CRC cells, in vitro experiments were conducted using human CRC cell lines HCT116 and SW480. Cells were cultured in DMEM medium supplemented with 10 % fetal bovine serum and 1 % penicillin–streptomycin at 37°C in a humidified incubator with 5 % CO₂.

### CCK-8实验

Cell proliferation was assessed using the Cell Counting Kit-8 (CCK-8) assay. When cells reached the logarithmic growth phase, HCT116 and SW480 cells were seeded into 96-well plates at a density of 5 × 10 ³ cells per well and exposed to DMEM containing different concentrations of Atrazine (0.1 μM and 0.5 μM), with untreated cells serving as the control group. After treatment, 10 μL of CCK-8 reagent was added to each well and incubated for an additional 1 h. The optical density was then measured at 450 nm.

### 细胞划痕实验

Cell migration ability was evaluated by wound healing assay. HCT116 and SW480 cells in the logarithmic growth phase were seeded in 6-well plates. When cell confluence reached 70 %–80 %, scratches were made perpendicularly across the cell monolayer using a 200 μL pipette tip. The wells were rinsed twice with PBS, and cells were then exposed to DMEM medium containing 0.1 μM or 0.5 μM Atrazine for 24 h. Wound closure was observed under a microscope, and the migration rate was quantified using ImageJ software.

### 实时荧光定量PCR

To verify changes in key gene expression after Atrazine exposure, quantitative real-time PCR (qPCR) was performed to detect mRNA levels in different treatment groups. After treatment with varying concentrations and time points of Atrazine, total RNA was extracted using TRIzol reagent and quantified with a NanoDrop 2000 spectrophotometer before being reverse-transcribed into cDNA. PCR amplification was conducted using a SYBR Green system, with β-actin serving as the internal control. The reaction conditions were as follows: pre-denaturation at 95°C for 30 s, followed by 40 cycles of 95°C for 5 s and 60°C for 30 s. Relative gene expression levels were calculated using the 2^−ΔΔCt method. All experiments were performed in triplicate. The primers were synthesized by Nanning Jienisi Biotechnology Co., Ltd., and their sequences are provided in Supplementary File 1.
