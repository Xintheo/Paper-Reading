### 第1段 句子标注

1. [疾病负担与流行] Acute kidney injury (AKI) represents a major global health burden characterized by high incidence and mortality [1,2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41905213/).
2. [疾病危险因素与病因] Renal ischemia-reperfusion injury (IRI) is one of the most prevalent etiologies of AKI, frequently occurring in clinical settings such as cardiac surgery, kidney transplantation, and shock.
3. [疾病治疗现状佐证] Current therapeutic options for IRI remain limited, underscoring the urgent need to elucidate its underlying molecular mechanisms to develop novel renal protective strategies [3](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41905213/).

### 第2段 逻辑链

疾病描述

### 第2段 句子标注

1. [疾病发病机制] The pathophysiology of IRI involves a complex interplay among tubular epithelial cell injury, metabolic dysregulation, and innate immune activation [4,5,6](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41905213/).
2. [疾病发病机制] Ischemic stress triggers an acute inflammatory response mediated by pattern recognition receptors, while simultaneously inducing profound metabolic reprogramming in renal tubular epithelial cells [7](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41905213/).
3. [疾病发病机制] This reprogramming is marked by a shift from fatty acid β-oxidation to glycolysis, leading to intracellular accumulation of lipids, particularly free fatty acids [8](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41905213/).
4. [疾病发病机制] These metabolites not only compromise cellular function but can also act as signaling molecules that activate immune cells, notably driving macrophages toward a pro-inflammatory phenotype [9](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41905213/).
5. [研究现状与缺口] Therefore, deciphering how tubular metabolic dysregulation controls the local immune microenvironment is a central scientific question in understanding the pathogenesis of IRI.

## 文献2 (PMID: 40139029)

### 第1段 逻辑链

生物分子物质描述

### 第1段 句子标注

1. [分子定义与归类] [Aspartame](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/pharmacology-toxicology-and-pharmaceutical-science/aspartame) is a synthetic [dipeptide](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/pharmacology-toxicology-and-pharmaceutical-science/dipeptide) that results from the reaction of l-aspartic acid with l-phenylalanine methyl ester, and it has become one of the most widely used [artificial sweeteners](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/pharmacology-toxicology-and-pharmaceutical-science/sweetening-agent) around the world [Choudhary and Pretorius, 2017](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325004257?via%3Dihub#bib8).
2. [分子理化性质] Due to its sweetness being approximately 200 times that of [sucrose](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/biochemistry-genetics-and-molecular-biology/sucrose) and its extremely low-calorie content, aspartame is widely used in sugar-free or low-sugar foods and beverages.
3. [研究现状与缺口] Although aspartame has gained widespread use in the market, its safety has been a subject of controversy for many years [Czarnecka et al., 2021](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325004257?via%3Dihub#bib9).
4. [分子与疾病关联] Studies have shown that aspartame and its metabolites can affect cognitive functions in [animal models](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/biochemistry-genetics-and-molecular-biology/animal-model) and humans, including issues with learning, [headaches](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/pharmacology-toxicology-and-pharmaceutical-science/headache), depression, and anxiety.
5. [分子与疾病关联] This may be closely related to the imbalance of excitatory and inhibitory [neurotransmitters](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/pharmacology-toxicology-and-pharmaceutical-science/neurotransmitter) caused by the [aspartic acid](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/biochemistry-genetics-and-molecular-biology/aspartic-acid) released from aspartame [Dar, 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325004257?via%3Dihub#bib11).
6. [分子与疾病关联] Secondly, aspartame can also activate [glutamate receptors](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/pharmacology-toxicology-and-pharmaceutical-science/glutamate-receptor), thereby inducing [neurotoxicity](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/pharmacology-toxicology-and-pharmaceutical-science/neurotoxicity) and neurostimulation[Shaher et al., 2023](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325004257?via%3Dihub#bib37).
7. [分子与疾病关联] Soheila Kashanian [Kashanian et al., 2013](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325004257?via%3Dihub#bib19) conducted in vitro experimental studies and found that aspartame interacts with native calf thymus DNA (CT-DNA), thereby affecting DNA's regulatory role in cellular structure and function.
8. [分子与疾病关联] Additionally, consuming aspartame during pregnancy can impair [placental growth](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/biochemistry-genetics-and-molecular-biology/placenta-development) in pregnant mice [Huang et al., 2023](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325004257?via%3Dihub#bib17).
9. [分子与疾病关联] Hojat has found that long-term consumption of aspartame leads to a decrease in [serum concentrations](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/biochemistry-genetics-and-molecular-biology/blood-level) of pituitary-testicular axis hormones and induces testicular [oxidative stress](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/biochemistry-genetics-and-molecular-biology/oxidative-stress) and cell apoptosis, thereby causing [male reproductive toxicity](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/biochemistry-genetics-and-molecular-biology/male-reproductive-toxicity) [Anbara et al., 2021](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325004257?via%3Dihub#bib2).

[分子与疾病关联] However, by increasing the production of [reactive oxygen species](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/pharmacology-toxicology-and-pharmaceutical-science/reactive-oxygen-metabolite) (ROS), aspartame may further contribute to carcinogenic risks [Shaher et al., 2023](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0147651325004257?via%3Dihub#bib37), including a significant impact on the liver. 6. [研究目的与假设] This multidisciplinary research strategy not only provides a scientific interpretation of the public health safety of artificial sweeteners but also opens new technical pathways for the toxicological research of aspartame.
7. [研究目的与假设] With the increasing use of artificial sweeteners by the population, the awareness of the safety and reliability of aspartame and other artificial sweeteners must be improved.
